AI EXPLORER — 6 AI mission games
CIY Club Myanmar × ScopeIT Education · Created by Coach Penny
=========================================================

SETTING UP A LAPTOP (5 minutes)
1. Copy the whole "ai-explorer" folder onto the laptop. Keep all files together.
2. Open the folder and double-click  index.html  (use Google Chrome).
3. Click the game you want to use. Press F11 for full screen.
4. No internet needed. Everything runs from the folder.

POSE PILOT (camera booth)
- The first time, Chrome asks to use the camera. Click "Allow".
- If the camera doesn't start: click the camera icon in the address bar,
  choose "Always allow", then reload the page.
- Best results: plain wall behind the child, good light, child stands
  1–2 steps back so arms are visible.

REAL OR AI? (needs pictures before the event)
- Put real photos in        images/real/
- Put AI-made pictures in   images/ai/
- Open  assets/realai-images.js  in Notepad and list each file name.
- At least 5 of each to start; 25+ of each is better.
- Tip: make pairs on the same subject (a real market photo + an
  AI market picture). Avoid photos showing children's faces.
- The day's score board stays on that laptop. A small link under the
  board lets volunteers clear it.

BETWEEN CHILDREN
Press "Next player" on the win screen. If a child walks away mid-game,
press F5 to restart.

LANGUAGE
Burmese is the default. The switch at the top right changes to English,
and the laptop remembers the choice.

MARKETING LINE
The menu and every win screen show a short "learn with CIY Club Myanmar &
ScopeIT Education" message. To change the contact line, edit BRAND at the
top of assets/common.js.
